# RC3.8D Scenario Science Stakeholder Foundation

Summary identity: `asklepios-rc3.8d-plain-language-change-summary`

This document explains the user-facing effect of the release. It does not replace the underlying assurance evidence.

## What changed?

- Added a twelve-scenario operational library with four genuinely different team-behavior profiles.
- Added a transparent eight-dimension source-conformance scorecard foundation.
- Added a treatment-admission status surface that keeps unreviewed concrete treatments inactive.
- Added hash-chained telemetry foundations for future timing and scoring calibration.
- Added Windows-portable release-test workspaces and a mandatory stakeholder capability map.

## What can a learner do now?

- Learners can browse scenarios by the kind of team challenge they must solve.
- Learners can see how future scorecards separate evidence dimensions and refuse to invent a score when evidence is incomplete.
- Learners can see that no concrete treatment choice is currently admitted.

## What can an instructor do now?

- Instructors can choose among direct-handoff, communications-relay, resource-coordination, and dual-constraint scenarios.
- Instructors can see each scenario reproducibility identity and behavior-equivalence class.
- Instructors receive a scorecard structure that preserves evidence and hard safety failures.

## What changed in scenario variety?

- The catalog contains twelve deterministic scenarios, three for each of four reviewed behavior profiles.
- Narrative, names, weather, or seed changes alone do not count as behavioral novelty.

## What changed in scoring?

- The score foundation separates situation assessment, information management, prioritization, communication, resource coordination, reassessment, closed-loop handoff, and safety.
- Critical safety events cannot be averaged away.
- Incomplete runs produce INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE instead of a fabricated percentage.

## What changed in treatment content?

- Two treatment areas are tracked through a monotonic admission pipeline.
- Zero concrete treatments are currently admitted or active.
- A treatment must complete evidence, applicability, role, effect, and independent-attestation gates before activation.

## What safety limitation remains?

- The system is for healthcare simulation and training only.
- Direct patient care and clinical decision support remain prohibited.
- No automatic treatment activation is permitted.

## What is still not calibrated or validated?

- Human-team behavior remains structurally modeled rather than empirically calibrated.
- Operational and clinical timing remain NOT_CALIBRATED.
- The scorecard is not a psychometrically validated proficiency measure.
- Patient dynamics remain source-bound static observations rather than validated continuous physiology.

## Permanent authority boundary

- Healthcare simulation and training are permitted only within the validated scope.
- Direct patient care is prohibited.
- Clinical decision support is prohibited.
- Patient-care authority is none.
- Operational timing is not calibrated.
- Scoring remains source-conformance only and is not a validated proficiency measure.
